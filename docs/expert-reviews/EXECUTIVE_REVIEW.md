# Executive Leadership Council Review — Autonomous Agency OS

---

## 1. CEO / Chief Executive Officer Review
*   **Current-State Assessment:** The agency seeks to scale revenue by productizing website delivery while building an internal OS to handle operational friction. The target market has been strategically narrowed from generic coaching institutes to vocational/IT training institutes in Vijay Nagar, Indore.
*   **Principal Findings:** The refined beachhead is strategically sound. IT and coding institutes run 12 batches a year, spend aggressively on digital ads, and directly understand conversion optimization. The greatest danger is premature over-engineering—building complex internal software before proving that local directors will actually write checks for ₹32,000.
*   **Verdict:** **`APPROVED SUBJECT TO MILESTONE ZERO`**. The founder must not spend more than 20 hours coding the OS until 2–3 paying clients are closed manually through direct field audits.

---

## 2. CPO / Chief Product Officer Review
*   **Current-State Assessment:** The product offering has been repositioned from "custom website development" to an **"Ad-Ready Student Funnel"** (Next.js speed + instant WhatsApp routing + local SEO + Headless CMS).
*   **Principal Findings:** This packaging solves the fatal flaw of custom web development: scope creep and price resistance. By fixing deliverables (1 high-speed landing page, 3 course pages, WhatsApp routing, 1 CMS portal, 2 revision rounds), delivery time is capped at 20–25 hours.
*   **Recommended Improvements:** Reject all client requests for custom student portals, complex payment gateways, or custom LMS features in the MVP.
*   **Verdict:** **`APPROVED`**.

---

## 3. CMO / Chief Marketing Officer Review
*   **Current-State Assessment:** Sourcing prospects and positioning the agency value proposition to Indian small business owners.
*   **Principal Findings:** Pitching "clean code, modern Next.js stack, or Tailwind" to a 45-year-old institute director in Indore fails 100% of the time. The winning narrative is financial: **"You spend ₹40,000/month on Google Ads. 45% of your mobile clicks bounce because your page takes 6 seconds to load. We recover those lost student admissions."**
*   **Regulatory Angle:** Leverage the Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) 2024 crackdown on deceptive education advertising. Help institutes build transparent, verified course landing pages that establish institutional authority.
*   **Verdict:** **`APPROVED`**.

---

## 4. CRO / Chief Revenue Officer Review
*   **Current-State Assessment:** Commercial deal structure, pricing, cash collection, and payment terms in Tier-2 India.
*   **Principal Findings:** In Tier-2 Indian commercial deals, post-paid invoices are routinely ignored or delayed by 60–90 days. Furthermore, monthly AMC retainers of ₹3,000–₹5,000 suffer high churn during cash-tight months.
*   **Mandatory Commercial Rules:**
    1. **Strict 50/50 Payment Milestones:** 50% advance before git init; 50% balance before production domain DNS pointing. Zero exceptions.
    2. **Quarterly Advance Billing for Retainers:** Never bill monthly. Retainers are billed at ₹10,500 every 3 months in advance.
    3. **Objection Handling (The ₹8,000 Freelancer):** When an owner says "A local boy can make a WordPress site for ₹8,000," reply: "An ₹8,000 WordPress template takes 6 seconds to load and leaks your ad budget. If you save just 1 student admission per month (worth ₹25,000 in fees), our system pays for itself on day 30."
*   **Verdict:** **`APPROVED WITH MANDATORY PAYMENT RULES`**.

---

## 5. CFO / Chief Financial Officer Review
*   **Current-State Assessment:** Financial sustainability, infrastructure operating expenses, and solo gross margins.
*   **Financial Model (Steady-State Month 6):**
    - *Revenue:* 2 new builds/mo (@ ₹35,000) + 10 active retainers (@ ₹3,500) = **₹1,05,000/month**.
    - *Hosting & Tools:* Vercel ($20) + Supabase ($0–$25) + Domains/n8n VPS ($10) = **~₹4,500/month ($55)**.
    - *Net Gross Margin:* **95.7%**.
    - *Net Monthly Operating Profit:* **~₹1,00,500/month**.
*   **Verdict:** **`HIGHLY VIABLE`**. Capital expenditure is near zero. Operational cash flow is positive from Client #1 due to the 50% advance policy.
