### Pass 2: Mechanistic Understanding of Indore Education Hubs
- Executed geographic search on Indore education ecosystems.
- Mapped primary clusters:
  - *Bhawar Kuan / Vishnu Puri:* Civil services (MPPSC/UPSC), state government exams, mass student PG hostels.
  - *Geeta Bhawan / South Tukoganj:* Corporate test-prep giants (Allen, Aakash, Resonance, CatalyseR) and legacy engineering/medical centers.
  - *Vijay Nagar / Palasia:* Commercial tech, vocational training, IT software development academies, and digital marketing centers.
- Discovered clear structural divergence between competitive test-prep (academic) and IT/vocational institutes.

### Pass 3: Market Economics & Local Agency Rate Cards
- Extracted commercial pricing benchmarks for web development in Indore across local agencies (CodingClave, SVSS, NexOpsDev, ExpertWebWorld).
- Established baseline market prices:
  - Single Landing Page: ₹4,000–₹15,000.
  - 5–10 Page WordPress Site: ₹20,000–₹45,000.
  - Dynamic Custom Site: ₹45,000–₹1,00,000.
  - Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC): Standardly pegged at 15%–20% of build cost per annum.

### Pass 4: Marketing Budget Behavior & Student Acquisition Dynamics
- Researched how coaching institutes in India allocate marketing funds during admission seasons.
- Findings: Institutes spend disproportionately on outdoor media (billboards, hoardings), newspaper jacket ads, and performance digital ads (Meta/Google Ads) to drive phone calls and WhatsApp leads.
- CCPA 2024 regulatory crackdown identified: Heavy fines levied on institutes for false topper claims and guaranteed selection promises, driving demand for verified, transparent institutional branding.

### Pass 5: Regulatory Compliance (DPDP Act, TRAI UCC, Meta/Google TOS)
- Investigated legal parameters of B2B cold outreach in India:
  - *DPDP Act 2023:* Personal data processing requires affirmative consent; no broad B2B commercial prospecting carve-out exists under statutory legitimate uses.
  - *TRAI TCCCPR 2018:* Commercial communication from standard personal SIMs is illegal; requires enterprise DLT registration.
  - *WhatsApp Business Policies:* Sending unsolicited marketing messages without prior affirmative opt-in causes rapid account suspension.
  - *Google Maps TOS (Sec 3.2.3):* Explicitly forbids scraping and caching place data for marketing databases.

### Pass 6: EdTech Vendor Sales Cycles & Decision-Maker Psychology
- Benchmarked B2B software and website sales motions with Indian institute owners (via TuitionDesk, Proctur studies).
- Key findings: Single-owner sole proprietorships; fast decisions when direct ROI on admissions is demonstrated; intense resistance to standalone tech features; demand for post-sale support and installment billing.

### Pass 7: Quantitative Modeling & Capacity Constraints

  - 40 hours/week total capacity.
  - Building a productized Next.js/CMS website: ~20–30 hours.
  - Ongoing monthly retainer support: ~2–3 hours/month per client.
  - Upper sustainable limit for solo founder: 8–12 active retainer clients + 1–2 new builds per month before hiring support staff.
