# Autonomous Agency OS — Research Brief

**Topic:** Market Hypothesis Validation for Autonomous Agency OS: Coaching & Skill-Training Institutes in Indore (Tier-2 India)  
**Primary Objective:** Empirically validate whether independent coaching and skill-training institutes in Indore represent an accessible, viable, and profitable initial beachhead market for a solo-founder productized website and digital-growth agency; establish realistic willingness to pay (WTP); map legal and platform-compliant acquisition channels; stress-test unit economics; and evaluate operational failure modes.  
**Domain:** Business Strategy, Agency Operations, Market Economics, Regulatory Compliance (India).  
**Geographic Scope:** Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India (focusing on commercial education hubs: Bhawar Kuan, Geeta Bhawan, Vijay Nagar, Palasia).  
**Time Horizon:** Current (2025–2026 operating environment) with historical and near-term regulatory projections (DPDP Act 2023, TRAI UCC, CCPA 2024).  
**Intended Use:** Commercial go-to-market decisions, pricing package design, compliant outreach protocol definition, and solo founder resource allocation.  
**Principal Investigator (Lead):** Research Orchestration & Final Synthesis Lead  

---

## 1. Problem Decomposition & Background

A solo software engineer/founder aims to build a productized digital agency supported by an internal AI-assisted operating system (`Autonomous Agency OS`). The platform is designed to automate routine business development (discovery, audit, outreach drafting, sales coordination, onboarding) so the founder can concentrate on website development, technical architecture, and quality assurance.

The master project brief posits two critical foundational hypotheses:
1. **Geographic Beachhead:** Starting in India, initially exploring Indore (Madhya Pradesh).
2. **Initial Niche Selection:** Independent coaching and skill-training institutes.

This research brief establishes the analytical framework required to validate, refine, or refute these hypotheses prior to capital or time expenditure.

## 3. Scope Boundaries

### In Scope
- Market segmentation of coaching vs. skill-training institutes in Indore.
- Local willingness to pay (one-time build vs. recurring monthly retainer).
- Direct local competitors and substitute solutions in Indore.
- Legal frameworks governing B2B cold outreach (DPDP Act 2023, TRAI UCC, Meta/WhatsApp policies, Google Maps TOS).
- Compliant acquisition playbooks feasible for a solo founder.
- Unit economic modeling of the agency under solo-operator constraints.

### Out of Scope
- National corporate coaching chains (Allen, Aakash, PhysicsWallah, FIITJEE) with centralized corporate marketing departments.
- Public SaaS architecture for external agencies.
- K-12 formal schools and universities (prolonged bureaucratic sales cycles).
