# Claim-Evidence Matrix

This matrix maps each foundational strategic claim evaluated during the research process to its primary sources, corroborating evidence, and calibrated epistemic state.

---

| Claim ID | Core Claim / Finding | Primary Sources | Epistemic State | Evidentiary Basis & Caveats |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CLM-01** | B2B cold email marketing without prior consent faces severe legal exposure in India. | SRC-01 (DPDP Act 2023, Sec 4, 7) | `VERIFIED` | The DPDP Act does not contain a blanket "legitimate interest" exemption for commercial prospecting. Processing personal data requires affirmative consent or narrow statutory exceptions. |
| **CLM-02** | Automated cold messaging via WhatsApp or unregistered SIMs violates telecom regulations and platform policies. | SRC-02 (TRAI TCCCPR), SRC-03 (WhatsApp Policy) | `VERIFIED` | TRAI prohibits commercial SMS/calls from standard 10-digit SIMs. WhatsApp Meta policies ban accounts sending unsolicited marketing without prior affirmative opt-in. |
| **CLM-03** | Scraping Google Maps / Google Places to build an agency outreach database violates platform terms. | SRC-04 (Google Maps TOS, Sec 3.2.3) | `VERIFIED` | Explicitly forbidden under Google developer terms; risks API project termination and IP blocking. |
| **CLM-04** | Prevailing market price for basic 5–10 page business websites in Indore ranges from ₹20,000 to ₹45,000. | SRC-06, SRC-07 | `CORROBORATED` | Multiple independent local agencies (CodingClave, SVSS, NexOpsDev) quote this range for standard WordPress/CMS sites. |
| **CLM-05** | Standalone maintenance contracts (AMC) in Indore small businesses command only ₹3,000–₹10,000 per year (₹250–₹850/mo). | SRC-06, SRC-07 | `CORROBORATED` | Quoted at 15%–20% of build cost. Selling a standalone maintenance retainer of ₹3,000–₹5,000/month requires bundling growth services. |
| **CLM-06** | Test-prep institutes in Bhawar Kuan have highly seasonal admissions and high price resistance for pure technical code. | SRC-08, SRC-09 | `CORROBORATED` | Revenue peaks around March–July and October. Founders prioritize hoardings, local flyers, and topper flex banners over clean code. |
| **CLM-07** | Skill-training institutes (IT, Coding, Digital Marketing) in Vijay Nagar exhibit year-round sales cycles and higher digital literacy. | SRC-09, SRC-10 | `CORROBORATED` | New batches launch every 4–6 weeks. These institutes rely heavily on Google Ads, Meta Ads, and fast landing pages to drive student walk-ins. |
| **CLM-08** | Pure cold email outreach to Indian coaching institute directors has low response rates (<1–3%). | SRC-08, Field Analyses | `PRELIMINARY` | Institute directors in Tier-2 India rarely monitor generic domain emails (`info@`); they conduct business on personal WhatsApp and mobile calls. |
| **CLM-09** | A hybrid acquisition model (direct in-person discovery / audit delivery in hubs + automated CRM follow-up) generates superior deal velocity. | SRC-08, Agency Operations Data | `INFERRED` | Indian SMB owners are relationship-driven and demand trust validation before committing ₹25k–₹50k to an independent agency. |
| **CLM-10** | A solo developer can sustainably support a maximum of 8–12 active monthly retainer clients while building new sites. | Agency Time-Motion Modeling | `INFERRED` | Assuming 15–20 hours/month per client for support, content updates, monitoring, and lead optimization alongside 20 hours/week for new builds. |
| **CLM-11** | Coaching institutes can be successfully closed on a pure automated self-serve checkout without human interaction. | Local Sales Benchmarks | `REFUTED` | High-touch owner negotiations, installment requests, and scope haggling are universal in Tier-2 commercial deals. |
